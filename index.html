01 / Research

Understanding change,
with communities.

I work at the intersection of climate adaptation, disaster resilience, and social inclusion.

With over six years of experience across India, the UK, and international climate fora, my work connects field research with policy advocacy and stakeholder engagement. I am particularly interested in the everyday experiences and knowledge of people living with environmental change.

Current doctoral project

(Re)Imagining Climate Futures

An ethnographic and creative exploration of Majuli Island, India. Working with flood-affected communities, I explore climate adaptation and community resilience through participatory, community-based research.

School of Geography, University of Leeds
Funded by an ESRC White Rose DTP 1+3 Studentship

Approach

Ethnography, qualitative inquiry, participatory and creative research.

Commitment

Gender equality, social inclusion, and community voices in climate action.
